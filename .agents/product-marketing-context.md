## Product

**Promise:** Target prospects worth the extra effort.

**One-liner:** Scorecard finds the companies that fit what you sell, and the person to talk to at each one, with the reasons why.

Describe businesses in plain language. Search narrows the market, Research checks facts and identifies the owner with sources, and Scoring ranks companies against the criteria the user values. The user decides who to approach and handles outreach. Scorecard is a self-serve prospecting product, not a consulting engagement.

## Competitive positioning

Justin's strategic shorthand: **a cheaper Clay, a smarter ZoomInfo, and a more AI-friendly tool.** This is the intended competitive direction, not a verified comparative performance claim.

**Customer-facing promise:** Find the companies you mean, without building a research workflow or sorting through a noisy database.

| Direction | Customer benefit | Evidence needed before comparative advertising |
|---|---|---|
| Cheaper Clay | Useful research with less workflow setup and lower total cost | Compare the same task, output quality, research/enrichment usage, setup effort and ongoing cost; subscription prices alone are insufficient. |
| Smarter ZoomInfo | Match what a business actually does, show fit evidence and reduce irrelevant results | Test the same queries against an agreed rubric; compare useful coverage and false positives. Do not imply universal data or contact superiority. |
| More AI-friendly | Describe a search naturally and, eventually, use Scorecard from the AI tools where work happens | Distinguish the natural-language product experience from agent access. API/MCP search, research, structured results and source links are a product direction until implemented and verified. |

Lead with outcomes in public copy. Competitor names can frame a customer's existing frustration without asserting untested savings or superiority. “AI inside the product” does not by itself mean an external AI agent can use it. Keep unsupported integrations out of launch promises.

## Audiences

- Sales teams selling to SMBs: fill territories with relevant accounts and people to approach.
- Founders and CEOs: test a segment before committing sales capacity.
- Acquisition searchers: build a thesis-fit shortlist with sourced ownership facts. Willingness to sell is unknown unless separately established.

Initial campaign hypothesis: focus organic demonstrations on founder-led B2B companies selling into narrow SMB markets. Keep acquisition examples distinct. The company buying Scorecard is not necessarily the kind of company being researched.

## Differentiation

- Describe what a business does rather than relying only on industry categories.
- Inspect evidence, source dates and missing facts.
- Choose fit criteria and see the reasons behind the ranking.
- Identify a person to approach where evidence supports it.
- Use a shortlist via CSV in existing workflows.

A sourced fact is not intent. Contact coverage is not universal. CSV compatibility is not a native CRM integration. Weighted scoring does not establish that feedback-trained scoring is shipped.

## Offer and primary action

The v20/v21 direction specifies a 14-day free trial with 500 credits; $59/month with 1,000 credits; $10 per 100 additional credits; custom Teams pricing and a pilot conversation. Confirm actual billing and product entitlements before publishing these terms.

Primary CTA: **Start free trial**. Next step: describe the businesses you want. Secondary actions: inspect a sample report, see how it works, or discuss a Teams pilot.

Owner/leader names identified from public sources are distinct from premium phone/email enrichment. v20/v21 place premium sources and contact details on the monthly plan. Verify before launch.

## Challenge offer and subscriber conversion

**Hook:** What companies are you struggling to find at scale?

**Offer:** Give us a query, geography and fit criteria. For the first ten accepted searches, get up to **100 candidate companies**, with the **10 strongest matches highlighted and evidence behind their fit**. Return fewer if the evidence does not support those counts; never fill the list with noise or call the top ten “A-grade” by default. Rehearse quality, cost and turnaround before publishing the promise.

The free deliverable includes the promised list and top-match evidence without a surprise signup gate. Candidate discovery is distinct from confirmed qualification: show research status, unknowns and the rubric, with the deepest manual review on the top matches.

The subscription buys repeatable access to the research capability, not gratitude for a free list:

1. Capture the real task, criteria, timing and whether the need recurs. Email for delivery is separate from an optional marketing subscription.
2. Deliver the list and ask which three they would contact and which are wrong.
3. Offer **Continue this search in Scorecard**. Preserve query, criteria and results using supported functionality; otherwise provide the exact query and a short walkthrough. No assumed saved-search/import capability.
4. Have the user refine criteria and generate their own useful shortlist during the trial.
5. Prompt a second real task: another territory, next account batch, another client or a new thesis.
6. Offer the normal subscription with verified pricing and actual trial expiry. Measure payment, repeat use and renewal separately from email opt-ins.

Do not start trials or create accounts before users choose signup. Do not make recurring manual research the paid offer. API/MCP access is not an advertised upgrade until available. Detailed drafts and fulfillment: `docs/plans/2026-11-13-prospect-search-challenge.md`.

## Launch direction confirmed by Justin

Justin wants organic content, email and eligible website audiences, rapid trial feedback, friends/family usability testing, colleague feedback and genuine testimonials, Scorecard-built target-account research, and later ads.

Do not make Justin's personal LinkedIn the primary channel or require him to be the public face. LinkedIn content should come from Scorecard or a real, willing representative/customer. Justin is comfortable appearing in private demos and participating personally on X, Reddit and Searchfunder. Branded screen-led demonstrations fit this preference; fabricated spokespeople do not.

Recommended execution: prove one recurring job in one segment to reach 10 paying accounts; repeat with less assistance to reach 30; expand the working channel toward 100 active paying accounts. Friendly testing and small Scorecard account batches run alongside the first organic examples, not as separate sequential programs. Email/pixel audiences support acquisition; they are not the primary success metrics. Small capped ads can recruit testers during discovery; expanding spend depends on activation, retention and economics.

The working report includes proposed diagnostic gates and tests for market relevance, message angle, observed pain versus generic account fit, and unassisted activation. These targets are hypotheses, not observed performance or benchmarks. Detailed plan: `docs/plans/2026-11-13-launch-ai-marketing-report.md`.

## AI's marketing job

Summarize evidence and feedback, draft from real demonstrations and founder stories, help research target accounts, and analyze cohort results. Use the existing marketing skills. Justin reviews claims, publishing, outreach, testimonials and spending. Do not invent stories or automate a marketing department before a manual loop works.

## Measurement

Proposed activation: complete a search in the user's own market, inspect a sourced answer, and save/export a shortlist the user regards as useful. Track organic, friendly testers, colleagues, Scorecard-sourced outreach and ads separately. Measure trials, activation, return use, paid conversion and reasons for stopping. Audience size and engagement are supporting signals, not revenue proof.

## Voice and proof

Plain, specific and evidence-led. Show a search, a fact and a useful next action. AI explains the mechanism; the shortlist is the outcome. Label example businesses/counts. Publish only genuine, approved testimonials; distinguish colleague feedback from independent paying-customer proof.

Do not restore retainer pricing, six-month service commitments, Growth Planning Intensives, embedded operators, managed CRM implementation or weekly consulting deliverables as the offer. Old documents and the legacy public site do not override the v20/v21 direction.
