Layer 01 / Foundation

The math

Start with a number the team can explain—not a target detached from the model.

  • Bottom-up revenue model
  • Segments and ideal customer profile
  • Pricing, packaging, and the pitch

Layer 02 / Operating system

Systems

Turn the model into visible operating rules the team can follow and maintain.

  • CRM stages and exit criteria
  • Fields that match the plan
  • One definition of qualified

Layer 03 / Capacity

Team

Translate the target into the people, coverage, and incentives required to carry it.

  • Hiring and ramp plan
  • Territories and account lists
  • Quotas and pay

Layer 04 / Weekly read

Reporting

Create a reliable weekly view that shows whether the system is working.

  • Funnel and activity reporting
  • Forecast and board pack
  • Win and loss reasons

Layer 05 / Leverage

Tools

Give reps more of the week back to selling and make the operating system easier to run.

  • Account scoring
  • Call preparation and research
  • Playbooks in the moment
  • Administration that runs itself
