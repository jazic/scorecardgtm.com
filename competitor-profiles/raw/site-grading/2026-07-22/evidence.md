# Competitive website grading — raw evidence

Date: 2026-07-22

This is a website-communication review, not a judgment of the underlying company. Evidence came from current homepages, linked case-study/review claims visible on those pages, and visual inspection of Scorecard, SalesHive, LeanScale, and Martal.

## Consulting and advisory

- Winning by Design: proprietary SPICED, Bowtie, and Revenue Architecture frameworks; diagnose/design/deploy offer; 4.8/5 from 750+ customers; claims 1 in 8 public SaaS companies; named outcome metrics.
- Kalungi: narrow B2B SaaS focus; GTM-as-a-service; 150+ SaaS companies; multiple named case studies with pipeline, ARR, MQL, and sales-cycle outcomes; three clear engagement models.
- RevPartners: Revenue Performance Model; fractional RevOps plus HubSpot/Clay implementation; dual Elite accreditation; 465+ HubSpot reviews; 1,000+ deployments.
- Skaled: embedded revenue-performance partner; cross-functional strategy and execution; 350,000+ hours and 1,000+ companies; testimonials on homepage are identified only by first name and initial.
- LeanScale: embedded AI-native GTM operators; strong customer logos and persona-specific pains; bespoke agents and playbooks; current homepage contains unfinished placeholder case-study copy.
- Predictable Revenue: established outbound authority; Humans + AI positioning; Four Funnels framework; execution and coaching offers; named testimonials, but homepage outcome counters and offer hierarchy are less crisp than category leaders.

## Managed outbound and hybrid services

- SalesHive: US-based SDRs operating on its own AI platform; 129K+ meetings, $2.5B+ pipeline, 2,285 clients; explicit human/platform division of labor; pricing and no long-term contracts visible.
- GrowthSpree: senior operators plus six named proprietary agents; 300+ B2B SaaS clients, $50M+ pipeline, 4.9/5 G2 claim, named campaign results; strong comparison against traditional agencies.
- Martal: 200+ sales executives, 15 years, proprietary AI outreach system, managed services plus AI platform; extensive customer and case-study library; homepage is long and information-dense.
- Callbox: Human + AI promise; 20 years, 15K+ companies, 10K+ campaigns, 60+ countries, large-enterprise logos and video testimonials; less obviously aimed at lower-mid-market buyers.
- K3C: market-entry specialization; consulting execution powered by its LeanGTM platform, also sold standalone; clear hybrid logic and testimonial, with less breadth of public proof than larger firms.
- SalesCaptain: experts plus custom Clay/AI workflows; 60+ organizations; process, case studies, and quantified outcomes; several aggressive claims reduce confidence.
- Belkins: explicit quantified promise, 1,000+ clients, rich named case studies, third-party review volume, transparent team structure; primarily a managed service rather than a product-led hybrid.
- Cleverly: clear done-for-you lead generation, 1,000 active clients, quantified leads/revenue/pipeline, pricing and reviews; headline and claim density feel more promotional than advisory.
- SalesRoads: concise promise, clear work split, case studies/reviews/ROI calculator and visible pricing; conventional managed service with little proprietary-system advantage.

## Tools and platforms

- lemlist: full outbound workflow, AI agents, role-based navigation, free trial and demo, customer stories, and 20,000+ sales teams; strong but feature-dense.
- Bizzy: highly specific European B2B data wedge; 50M+ companies, 1,000+ teams, product workflow, case studies, testimonials, and free trial.
- Sortlist: unusually sharp agency persona; intent signals plus marketplace plus outbound; recognizable brand logos and concrete workflow; two-sided marketplace adds conceptual complexity.
- ColdIQ: focused developer/agent proposition; 40+ providers, 700+ endpoints, one API, natural-language workflow; 300+ clients and $50M+ ARR generated claim; current developer-first pivot narrows the audience.
- YourMax: sharp high-intent-lead promise, ICP-to-signal-to-enriched-lead workflow, 600+ sales teams; proof is thinner and some illustrated lead data is visibly demonstrative.
- Overloop: clear AI prospecting promise, free signup, agency portal and audience use cases; weaker outcome proof and a broader, less differentiated story.

## Scorecard current homepage

- Hero: “Know how you’ll grow and which accounts will get you there.”
- Offer: growth plan, account sourcing and scoring, and fractional outbound.
- System: plan → accounts → action → learning.
- Visible strengths: strong visual design, executive-level problem framing, clear buyer paths, industry and CRM fit, distinct service options, client login.
- Visible gaps: no named customers, testimonials, case studies, third-party reviews, team credentials, or validated outcome metrics on the homepage; product/platform is described but not concretely demonstrated above the fold.
