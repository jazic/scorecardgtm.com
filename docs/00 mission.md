# ScoreCard GTM — Mission

## Mission Statement

Scorecard is a managed AI prospecting service that analyzes your sales history, discovers your real ICP, builds and tests a custom scoring model, and delivers a recurring pipeline of prequalified prospects that gets sharper every month.

We learn your ideal customer, then keep finding more of them.

## What We Believe

Every business knows what a great customer looks like, or thinks they do. The problem is that gut instinct about ICP often drifts from what the data shows. And even when the ICP is clear, finding more of them at scale without drowning the sales team in unqualified noise is a different problem entirely.

Generic lead databases, purchased lists, and keyword-based searches all produce volume without precision. They treat every prospect the same.

Scorecard exists because your definition of "fit" is unique, and your prospecting system should reflect that.

We work with each client to surface patterns in the accounts they already love — the customers they wish they had ten more of — translate those patterns into an AI-powered scoring model, source candidates from the open web and professional networks, score every prospect against the client's specific criteria, and deliver a ranked pipeline of prequalified prospects that compounds in accuracy based on real sales outcomes.

## How We Work

Scorecard operates in three phases per client engagement:

**Define** — We start with your accounts. You hand us a list of customers you'd like to see more of — typically a Salesforce export of won opportunities, or a hand-picked CSV of "good accounts" — and we run an AI-assisted clustering analysis to surface the cohorts hiding inside that list. One upload often yields multiple distinct ICPs (a single sales motion may serve two or three real customer types you've been treating as one). We work with your team to promote the clusters worth pursuing into structured scoring rubrics that blend hard filters with AI-classified soft signals. For clients without historical accounts to share, we build the ICP directly from your team's expertise and known examples.

**Test** — We source a small batch of prospects, score them against the rubric, and put them in front of you. You tell us what's right, what's wrong, and what's missing. We adjust. We iterate until the model reflects your judgment, not ours. Only prequalified prospects (those clearing the score threshold) make it to your team.

**Scale** — Once calibrated, we deliver prequalified prospects on a recurring cadence. Results land where your team already works (Salesforce, Slack, dashboard, API). Every month, we refine the scoring model based on what your sales team actually closes, disqualifies, or discovers. The system compounds.

## Who We Serve

Scorecard is built for companies with:

- A sales team that needs qualified pipeline, not raw lists
- A specific or discoverable ICP that generic tools can't capture
- A willingness to invest in calibration upfront for compounding returns over time
- A minimum 6-month engagement horizon

## What We Are Not

- We are not a data vendor. We do not sell lists.
- We are not a CRM. We push results into yours.
- We are not a one-time service. The value compounds with time and feedback.
- We are not self-serve software. We operate the system on your behalf, with your input at every calibration point.

## Core Principle

The scoring model is the product. Everything else (sourcing, delivery, dashboards, analysis) is infrastructure in service of one goal: making sure the next name your sales team calls is the right one.
