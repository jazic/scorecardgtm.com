# Scorecard website and positioning brief

Updated November 13, 2026. Direction confirmed by Justin: use homepage v20/v21; retire managed-service positioning.

## Product and promise

Scorecard is a self-serve prospecting product for people who sell to, or buy, small and mid-sized businesses. Describe the businesses you want. Search finds candidates, Research checks the facts and identifies the owner, and Scoring ranks the fit with reasons. People decide who to contact and do the outreach.

**Headline:** Target prospects worth the extra effort.

**Supporting copy (v21):** Scorecard finds the companies that fit what you sell, and the person to talk to at each one, with the reasons why.

The customer buys a usable shortlist: the right companies, evidence of fit, and the person to approach. AI explains how the work happens; it is not a substitute for the customer outcome.

## Competitive positioning

Justin's strategic shorthand: **a cheaper Clay, a smarter ZoomInfo, and a more AI-friendly tool.** This is the intended competitive direction, not a verified comparative performance claim.

**Customer-facing promise:** Find the companies you mean, without building a research workflow or sorting through a noisy database.

| Direction | Customer benefit | Evidence needed before comparative advertising |
|---|---|---|
| Cheaper Clay | Useful research with less workflow setup and lower total cost | Compare the same task, output quality, research/enrichment usage, setup effort and ongoing cost; subscription prices alone are insufficient. |
| Smarter ZoomInfo | Match what a business actually does, show fit evidence and reduce irrelevant results | Test the same queries against an agreed rubric; compare useful coverage and false positives. Do not imply universal data or contact superiority. |
| More AI-friendly | Describe a search naturally and, eventually, use Scorecard from the AI tools where work happens | Distinguish the natural-language product experience from agent access. API/MCP search, research, structured results and source links are a product direction until implemented and verified. |

Lead with outcomes in public copy. Competitor names can frame a customer's existing frustration without asserting untested savings or superiority. “AI inside the product” does not by itself mean an external AI agent can use it. Keep unsupported integrations out of launch promises.

## Buyers and jobs

| Buyer | Job | Useful output |
|---|---|---|
| Sales teams selling to SMBs | Fill a territory | Ranked accounts by territory, reasons and people to approach |
| Founders and CEOs | Test the next segment | Counts, sample accounts and unknowns before investing in a sales hire |
| Acquisition searchers | Build a thesis-fit shortlist | Relevant businesses and sourced ownership history; willingness to sell remains unknown |

Initial launch recommendation: concentrate on founder-led B2B companies selling into a narrowly defined SMB market. This is a proposed campaign focus, not a change to the broader product audience.

## What the homepage must demonstrate

1. Describe a specific market in everyday language.
2. Narrow it to the territory the buyer can serve.
3. Open a company and inspect a signal's source and date.
4. Identify the person to approach, with missing information explicit.
5. Change what matters and inspect the ranking and reasons.
6. Export the shortlist or use it in the customer's existing workflow.

Use one coherent example across Search, Research and Scoring. v21 is the visual/product narrative baseline; v20 supplies supporting detail. The competitive positioning and challenge offer in this brief supersede earlier strategy wording; the HTML mockups have not been edited. Example companies and counts must stay labeled as examples. Screenshots and mockups do not prove shipped functionality.

## Offer and conversion

The v20/v21 offer is a 14-day free trial with 500 credits; the monthly plan is $59 with 1,000 monthly credits and $10 per 100 additional credits. Teams have custom pricing and a pilot conversation. These are the approved marketing direction, subject to checking the actual product and billing before publication.

Primary CTA: **Start free trial**. Next action: describe the businesses you want. Secondary proof action: view a sample report or see how it works. Teams CTA: discuss a pilot.

Names identified from sources are distinct from phone/email enrichment. Premium sources and owner contact details require the monthly plan; contact coverage is not guaranteed. CSV export is the stated CRM path. Do not imply native integrations from export compatibility.

## Challenge offer and subscriber conversion

**Hook:** What companies are you struggling to find at scale?

**Offer:** Give us a query, geography and fit criteria. For the first ten accepted searches, get up to **100 candidate companies**, with the **10 strongest matches highlighted and evidence behind their fit**. Return fewer if the evidence does not support those counts; never fill the list with noise or call the top ten “A-grade” by default. Rehearse quality, cost and turnaround before publishing the promise.

The free deliverable includes the promised list and top-match evidence without a surprise signup gate. Candidate discovery is distinct from confirmed qualification: show research status, unknowns and the rubric, with the deepest manual review on the top matches.

The subscription buys repeatable access to the research capability, not gratitude for a free list:

1. Capture the real task, criteria, timing and whether the need recurs. Email for delivery is separate from an optional marketing subscription.
2. Deliver the list and ask which three they would contact and which are wrong.
3. Offer **Continue this search in Scorecard**. Preserve query, criteria and results using supported functionality; otherwise provide the exact query and a short walkthrough. No assumed saved-search/import capability.
4. Have the user refine criteria and generate their own useful shortlist during the trial.
5. Prompt a second real task: another territory, next account batch, another client or a new thesis.
6. Offer the normal subscription with verified pricing and actual trial expiry. Measure payment, repeat use and renewal separately from email opt-ins.

Do not start trials or create accounts before users choose signup. Do not make recurring manual research the paid offer. API/MCP access is not an advertised upgrade until available. Detailed drafts and fulfillment: `docs/plans/2026-11-13-prospect-search-challenge.md`.

## Proof and claims

Show the source, observation date, fit criteria, reasons and unknowns. Verify index coverage, source access, pricing, credits, scoring behavior, export and signup before making availability claims. Do not promise every business is discoverable, every owner is identifiable, or every fit is ready to buy. A sourced fact is not buying intent.

Do not bring back retainers, a six-month engagement minimum, Growth Planning Intensives, embedded operators, managed CRM implementation or weekly consulting deliverables as the core offer. Do not reuse fictional testimonials or demo metrics as customer proof.

## Voice and page sequence

Plain, specific, evidence-led. Lead with the task and output. Explain Search → Research → Score through an actual example. Prefer facts to agent theatrics or claims about replacing a department.

Page sequence: promise → three buyer jobs → Search → Research → Score → evidence → workflow/export → pricing → questions → trial.

## Launch sequence

Justin wants organic acquisition, email/eligible pixel audiences, friendly and colleague testing, Scorecard-built account lists, and later ads. Execute these as a focused progression: first 10 paying accounts in one segment, repeat the motion to 30, then expand toward 100 active paying accounts. Bring small target-account batches into the first stage; keep friendly testing parallel. Do not use Justin's personal LinkedIn as the launch channel. Use Scorecard-branded demonstrations there; Justin may participate personally on X, Reddit and Searchfunder and appear in private demos. Use the detailed working report below for hypotheses, milestones, tests and retention gates.
