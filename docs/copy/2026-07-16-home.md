## How it works

Three agents do the research: the right companies, the facts about each one, and the person to talk to. You decide who to call.

- **The Search agent sizes your market.** Describe the businesses you want in plain words, then narrow to the part you can serve.
- **The Research agent checks the facts and finds the owner.** Inspect the sources behind the answers. No evidence, no answer.
- **The Scoring agent puts the best fits first.** Choose what matters. Inspect the reasons and the person to approach.

## Proof and workflow

Every answer shows where it came from. Missing facts remain unknown. Export a shortlist to CSV; research existing accounts; size a market using a stated method and date. Keep illustration labels visible. Validate these product claims before release.
