**The outline**

It is specific about presentation and too loose about proof. “Four sticky keyframes” dictates execution before establishing what each scene must demonstrate.

Frame each section around **visitor question → current capability → visible evidence → takeaway**. Keep the recurring business and visual language as constraints. Make animation optional, with a complete static version for mobile and reduced motion. Replace “cheap to keep” with “I understand what my usage will cost.”
