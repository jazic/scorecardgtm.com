# Homepage storytelling outline

Updated November 13, 2026. Baseline: `archive/2026-11-14-cleanup/mockups/home-v21.html`, supported by v20. Positioning source: `../2026-07-16-website-brief.md`.

## Updated positioning

Find the companies you mean, without building a research workflow or sorting through a noisy database. Internal competitive direction: cheaper Clay, smarter ZoomInfo, more AI-friendly; comparative claims and API/MCP availability require proof. The v20/v21 mockups remain the product/design baseline, not proof that agent integrations are shipped.

The challenge is an acquisition entry point: query → up to 100 candidates → strongest 10 with evidence → continue the same search in the trial → a second useful task → subscription. Keep the main site's trial CTA and the challenge's “Send your search” CTA appropriate to their respective pages. Deliver the promised list without surprise gating.

## The argument

Scorecard finds companies that fit what you sell and the person to approach, with the reasons why. Describe a market; inspect sourced facts; prioritize the shortlist. Your team does the outreach.

| Section | Visitor's question | Demonstration |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | What do I get? | Relevant company, fit evidence and person to approach |
| Buyer jobs | Is this for me? | Fill a territory, test a segment, build an acquisition shortlist |
| Search | Can it find my specific market? | Plain-language description narrowed to a territory |
| Research | Can I trust the result? | Source and date behind each answer; owner identified; unknowns visible |
| Score | Who deserves attention first? | Explicit weights, ranking and reasons |
| Evidence | Where does this come from? | Source coverage and methodology with qualified claims |
| Workflow | Can I use the shortlist? | CSV export, existing-account research and market sizing |
| Pricing | What does trying it cost? | 14-day/500-credit trial, $59 monthly plan, Teams pilot |
| Questions and signup | What happens next? | Start a trial, describe the businesses you want |

Use one example across the three agents. Keep example counts and companies labeled. AI research and ranking support human decisions; no autonomous outreach promise. Do not conflate weighted scoring with future learning from user feedback. Verify feature availability before launch.

The earlier v15 learning-from-marks narrative is historical. The launch does not depend on that capability or a managed consulting engagement.
