**B. Keep the example, but say who “you” are.**

Landscaping plus snow removal is understandable and demonstrates a distinction an industry category misses. The missing piece is why a B2B buyer cares.

Add this setup:

> **Suppose you sell scheduling software to contractors who manage landscaping and commercial snow removal. You want to find prospects in California.**

Then carry that buying context through the ranking. Commercial work and year-round services have an intelligible connection to the seller’s needs. “Owns its trucks,” “2 yards,” and “active on social” need a reason to matter—or should leave the example.

Also replace “That’s your total market” with “These are the matching businesses we found.” Search results alone don’t establish a complete market.
