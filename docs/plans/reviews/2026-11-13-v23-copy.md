## Page-level story check
- **What it is, in 5 seconds:** Mostly clear. The headline is about benefit and the subhead explains the mechanism. The card and map land the idea. The page should also say "AI" or "agents" once above the fold, because the agents are the mechanism and they don't appear until section 4.
- **Who it's for:** It's clear by section 2. The hero never names the buyer type (small businesses as the target market). Optional eyebrow: **For teams that sell to local and owner-run businesses.** (Confirm this matches the ICP.)
- **Why trust it:** Good. "No source, no answer," the list-vs-Scorecard comparison and "What we can't tell you" carry trust honestly without customer proof. The example labels have to be consistent, or the whole page reads as staged.
- **What to do next:** Clear: Start free trial. It weakens in the proof footer and the FAQ ("Book a demo").
- **Flow:** It works, but the example breaks twice: the intro says snow contractors and the score list has non-landscapers. Make it one continuous story, sentence → 41,300 → 412 → Sierra Line → Dana.
