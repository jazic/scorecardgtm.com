# Homepage v16: consolidated review

Three independent adversarial reviews of `mockups/home-v16.html` (Impeccable critique, Claude Opus high, Codex gpt-6-astra medium). Full reviews sit next to this file.

## All three agree

1. **The agent isn't doing anything visible.** It's a small pill under the picture; the dots and bars move by themselves. Fix: the agent is the headline subject ("The Search agent checks…") and a visible mark *inside* the picture (the Scorecard dot mark, or an ink bracket/lens) that sweeps, reads and carries.
2. **Too many layers per section.** Step rail + headline + body + visual + pill + progress ticks + caption all say one thing. Cut the rail, ticks and the 12 captions.
3. **Ranking happens three times.** Search already shows "38 match best", Signals plots weaker → stronger, then Score ranks. Search should only find (in or out), Signals only facts, Score the only place order appears.
4. **Signals never shows a source.** The highlighted "commercial snow removal" quote (the best moment from earlier versions) is gone. End Signals on one pill opened to its quote, site and date.
5. **Signals and Score read as dashboards** (histogram with axis labels, stacked bars with a legend; bar lengths don't explain 94 vs 88).
6. **Too much pinned scrolling** (~12.6 screens before pricing) and unreadable on mobile (visuals scaled to 55%).
7. **Copy overclaims:** "accounts that won't buy" (we score fit, not buying intent), "Send to your CRM" (it's CSV), contact row + Call button promote premium data next to a free CTA, invented counts look measured.
8. **TAM/SAM belongs inside Search** (a "Size this market" moment), not a separate section. The US database is the universe; TAM = all qualifying businesses nationally; SAM = the states/segments you can serve. Label counts illustrative.

## Cut list (union, agreed by at least two reviewers)
Step rails · progress ticks · all 12 captions · axis labels · Score legend · hand notes that only repeat the copy (keep one per section at most) · "Most teams" badge · roadmap/"Coming soon" rows in the pricing comparison · Why section's headings and blurbs (keep audience name + example) · hero "Dana Kessler / Call" row · duplicate audience nav links · coral dot in And more · footer tagline · sign-up dot wallpaper (Astra only).

## Split opinions
- **Hero headline.** Opus/Astra want to replace "Target prospects worth the extra effort" ("worth your next conversation"); Impeccable keeps the hero. Founder's call.
- **Hand notes.** Astra cuts all; Impeccable keeps coral for human moments; Opus cuts all three in the agent sections.
- **Scroll.** Impeccable: shorten to 250–300vh. Astra: drop the scroll runway, show a finished still with optional replay.

## Contradictions to fix
9,840 in keyframes vs 9,830 on page · "38 match best" vs "top 5 of 412" · "Hiring now" pill missing from Sierra's list · hero promises "what your best deals have in common" (coming-soon learning) · FAQ "Standard Signals" vs pricing ticks Custom Signals on trial · "Active on social" vs LinkedIn being premium · "Next, you'll describe your best customers" excludes acquisition searchers · coral buttons fail contrast (3.3:1).

## Recommended v17 plan
1. Agent becomes the subject: headlines "The Search agent…", "The Research agent…", "The Scoring agent…", and the dot mark moves inside each picture.
2. Search: find only (grey → blue, no "best"). Add "Size this market" as its opening beat (US → TAM → SAM → your states).
3. Signals: one business, sources visibly becoming answers; end on an opened pill with its quote.
4. Score: keep the pill heap → tiers beat; end on 5 plain rows (grade, name, top reason, score). No legend, no bars.
5. Cut list above; 250–300vh per section; still frames on mobile.
6. Copy fixes from the contradictions list.
