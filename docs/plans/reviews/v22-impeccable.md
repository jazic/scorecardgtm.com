## Top 8 issues by impact

**1. No sentence says what Scorecard is.** The H1 is a slogan. The subhead ("finds the companies that fit what you sell") could describe any data vendor. A cold visitor can't tell what they type, what comes back, or what kind of product this is. *Hurts:* visitors who can't place the product leave before How it works. *Fix:* keep the H1 and make the subhead concrete and mechanical: "Describe who you sell to in plain words. Scorecard searches 33.2M US businesses, checks each one against your criteria, and gives you a ranked shortlist with the owner's name and a source for every fact." Add a static example query above the hero card ("landscapers that also plow snow").

**2. "Three jobs Scorecard does well" is about us, and it hedges.** "Does well" invites the question "and badly?" The headline also doesn't match the nav label "Who it's for". *Hurts:* the first section after the hero is where visitors pick themselves out, and this one is talking about the product. *Fix:* "Whoever picks the next account, it starts with a shortlist." Shorter option: "Built for the person choosing who to call next."

**3. "Fill a territory" and "Test your next segment" are jargon and promise volume.** "Fill" sounds like quantity, the opposite of "worth the extra effort". "Test a segment" doesn't say what you learn. *Fix:* Sales teams: **"Give every rep a list worth calling."** (keep "412 California accounts → 4 reps × 103"). Founders: **"Size a market before you hire for it."** Searchers: **"Find owner-run companies that fit your thesis."** Also make the rep dots show ranking (darker means better fit), so the visual says "ranked" and not just "divided".

**4. The How it works subhead lists features.** "Three agents do the research: the right companies, the facts…" describes the machine. *Fix:* lead with what you get: "Describe your customer in a sentence. Get back a ranked shortlist: why each company made it, where each fact came from, and who to ask for. You decide who to call." The agents can introduce themselves in their own sections.

**5. The proof section isn't proof.** "Every" and "None" set as giant stat numerals are words posing as numbers. Both repeat the Research agent's "No evidence, no answer" a screen earlier. 33.2M and 8 describe capability, not results. *Hurts:* this band should lower risk before pricing, and right now it repeats claims. *Fix:* retitle it **"Check our work."** Show one opened sample record (three facts, each with source and date, plus one "not found" row) next to the source chips. The single link should be "See the full sample report →". Move 33.2M to a footnote under Search. When the launch plan's step 3 happens, replace this band with "We built our own target list with Scorecard. Here it is." That will beat any stat.

**6. "Put your shortlist to work" doesn't read as "there's more".** The title sounds like a fourth step, and the three large illustrated cards give it the same weight as the main sections. "Size a market for your board" repeats the founders card. *Fix:* headline **"More you can do with it."** Make it a lighter, compact list without big illustrations: "Score the accounts already in your CRM", "Add your own Signals" (in the pricing table), "Export to any CRM or spreadsheet". Cut the board card, or fold "with method and date, ready for a board deck" into the founders card.

**7. Mobile pricing is broken.** At 390px the table scrolls sideways. Prices and the Monthly CTA are off-screen, and the Monthly highlight shows up as a stray blue strip over the label column. On mobile the narrator bar also squeezes the agent name and a five-line message side by side, and the Research diagram's labels drop to about 7px. *Fix:* below 720px, stack the plans as three cards with Monthly first. Put the narrator label above the text. Hide the source cards in the Research diagram and keep the record and quote.

**8. Small inconsistencies that cost trust.** The CSV card says "2+ locations" but Signals says "2 yards". The SAM counter reads 403 while the narrator says 412, so finish the count-up by p≈0.8. Teams lists "Extra credits: Included" and "Credits: Scaled to your market" together. The nav says "Who it's for" but the section is titled "jobs". Pick one term for each.

## Keep

- The snow story running through hero, Search, Research and Score, with one set of facts.
- Narrator bars: first-person, short, and showing work.
- The opened quote with "checked Nov 4", "Revenue · not found", and the dashed "Phone & email · monthly plan" pill. These are the most credible moments on the page.
- The Score list with reasons and "Person to approach" on the top row.
- "14 days · 500 credits" under the hero CTA, and the FAQ line "Agents research and rank; your team does the outreach."
