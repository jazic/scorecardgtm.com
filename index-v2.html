● How it works

Three pillars. One model that gets smarter.

The whole thing pivots on one idea: most prospects are noise, a few are the signal. We find the signal hiding in your won deals, and the model improves each month you feed back what closed.

D
01 · Define

Cluster your wins.

We start with your last 30–150 closed-won deals. We cluster them by firmographic and behavioral signal. Most teams find 2–4 distinct ICPs hiding inside what they thought was one sales motion.

  • Segment definitions delivered in week 1
  • Plain-language rubric, not a black box
  • Side-by-side comparison to your stated ICP
T
02 · Test

Score a small batch.

We score 500–2,000 prospects against your v0 model and tier them A–D. You walk through the results with us. We tune until the model reflects your team's judgment, not ours.

  • One calibration session in week 2–3
  • Every score is explainable, line by line
  • You sign off before anything goes recurring
S
03 · Scale

Monthly delivery. The model keeps tuning.

Once it's calibrated, we deliver a batch of A/B-tier prospects each month into your CRM, Slack, or a hosted dashboard. We refit on your latest closed deals. Wins move it tighter, losses tell us what to drop.

  • Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, or dashboard
  • Monthly drift report: what changed and why
  • The model improves over time instead of going stale
● Method

Won deals are the ground truth.

Most ICP exercises start with a brief and end with a slide. Ours starts with what your team closed last quarter and ends with a model your reps can argue with.

01

Won deals are ground truth.

The model fits to the deals you actually closed, not a brief or a persona doc. If marketing's ICP and sales' reality don't agree, the deals are right.

02

Every score is explainable.

If you disagree with an A, we can show you the rubric and the data behind it. If you're right, the model updates. There's no "trust the algorithm" step.

03

The model recalibrates monthly.

Wins tighten the fit. Losses tell us what to drop. We send a one-page drift report each month so you can see what changed without having to ask.

04

No CRM mirror, no black box.

We don't ingest your CRM wholesale. We use the firmographic fields the model needs and nothing else. Your data stays where it lives.

● Questions

What people ask before the intro.

If yours isn't here, ask on the call. We'd rather show the model than pitch it.

How is this different from a list provider?+
A list provider sells you names. We build a model from your won deals, score prospects against it, and refit it each month based on which scored prospects actually became opportunities. Lists go stale. The model gets sharper.
What do you need from us to start?+
A list of your last 30–150 won deals (company name and close date is enough), and a 60-minute kickoff. We build the v0 model, score a small batch, and you tell us where it's right and where it's wrong. You don't need to plug us into your CRM to start.
How long until we see scored prospects?+
First batch in week 1–2. Calibration session in week 3. Monthly delivery starts in week 4. You'll have a working model on your data inside a single sprint.
What's the pricing?+
Flat monthly retainer, scoped to your batch volume. We don't do per-lead pricing or platform fees. We'll quote on the intro call once we understand the motion. Most engagements land between $4–12k/month.
Do you replace our SDRs?+
No, we feed them. We deliver prospects ranked by fit, your team does the outreach. Most customers see their SDRs get more efficient because they're working a sharper top-of-funnel instead of grinding through a noisy list.
What if we already have an ICP defined?+
Good. That's our hypothesis to test. Often the ICP on paper diverges from what your team actually closes. We either confirm it fast or find the segments hiding inside it. Either outcome is useful.
Where does our data live?+
We deliver into whatever your team already uses: Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, or a hosted dashboard. The model lives in our infrastructure. We don't take a copy of your CRM, and won-deal exports stay scoped to the firmographic fields we need.
