Last updated: [Date]

This policy explains what information [Scorecard legal entity name] ("Scorecard", "we", "us") collects through scorecardgtm.com (the "site") and the Scorecard app at app.scorecardgtm.com (the "service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email hello@scorecardgtm.com.

## Information you give us

- **Forms on the site.** When you use the contact form or send us a search through the challenge form, we collect your name, email address, company (if you give it), and what you write: your message, or your search, geography and criteria.
- **Your account.** When you sign up for the service, we collect your name, email address and sign-in details.
- **What you put into the service.** The searches you run, the criteria and Signals you define, lists you upload, and notes you add.
- **Billing.** If you subscribe, our payment processor, [payment processor], collects your payment details. We don't store full card numbers.
- **Messages.** If you email us, we keep the email and our reply.

## Information we collect automatically

- **Analytics and session recordings.** We use PostHog to understand how people use the site: pages viewed, clicks, referring site, device and browser type, and approximate location derived from your IP address. PostHog also records sessions, a reconstruction of how the page looked and what was clicked, so we can find what's confusing. Text typed into form fields is masked and is not recorded. PostHog stores an identifier in your browser's cookies or local storage to recognize return visits.
- **Hosting and security logs.** The site runs on Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and request details to deliver pages and protect against abuse.
- **Fonts.** The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when it fetches them.
