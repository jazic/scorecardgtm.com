Questions

Something else? Book a demo and ask us.

What happens after the 14-day trial?
Pick the monthly plan to keep going. The trial covers Search, Scores and Standard Signals. LinkedIn, Google Places and owner contacts need the monthly plan.
How is this different from buying a list?
A list gives you businesses in an industry code. Scorecard reads each one, checks it against what you described, and shows why it ranks where it does.
Do the agents contact anyone?
No. Agents research and rank; your team does the outreach.
What contact details are included?
Owner and leader names come from public records and websites. Phone and email are premium reads on the monthly plan, and not every business has them.
