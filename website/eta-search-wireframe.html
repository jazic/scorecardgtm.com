Signals worth noticing in commercial landscaping

A signal is evidence to investigate, not a universal verdict. Scorecard interprets each one in the context of your thesis.

A credible website

Reveals service mix, commercial focus, certifications, customers, leadership, and territory.

Little digital presence

May reveal an overlooked, referral-driven operator with room for professionalization.

Multiple locations

Can indicate operating scale, route density, management depth, and a repeatable branch model.

Recurring commercial work

Property-manager language, maintenance programs, and seasonal contracts suggest repeat revenue.

Management beyond the owner

Branch managers, operations leaders, estimators, and crew supervisors can reduce owner dependence.

Not every owner should get the same outreach.

A clear tier gives your team an operating decision, not another data point.

A

Personal campaign

Bespoke research, personal mail, direct calls, and persistent long-term follow-up.

B

High-touch campaign

Personalized multichannel outreach with a structured follow-up cadence.

C

Thesis test

Lower-cost outreach used to test the segment and gather more evidence.

D

Hold or exclude

Poor fit, insufficient evidence, wrong model, duplicate, franchise, or subsidiary.

The score is explainable. Every ranking shows the supporting evidence, what remains unknown, and the next question worth answering.

The judgment layer

Use the sources you already have. Make better decisions with them.

  • Not another company database. Bring lists from directories, data providers, associations, interns, or your own research.
  • Not a black-box seller prediction. Keep acquisition fit, outreach priority, and evidence confidence separate.
  • Not generic sales scoring. The rubric is built around the business you want to acquire and operate.
  • Not a static screen. Refine the criteria as owner conversations and search results challenge the thesis.
