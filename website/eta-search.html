Practical search applications

Use Scorecard when the list still leaves you with too much work.

Databases can produce a broad universe. Scorecard helps when the real buy box depends on business model, customer mix, local presence, owner dynamics, or other details that filters miss.

Map the search

Build a usable market, not another raw list.

Combine directories, data providers, association lists, and your own research into one deduplicated universe that can be screened consistently.

Remove the lookalikes

Separate real fits from companies that only look right.

Distinguish commercial maintenance from residential or project work, service from installation, and independent operators from franchises or subsidiaries.

Prioritize the owners

Find the few companies worth personal pursuit.

Identify the businesses that justify deeper research, a tailored letter, a direct call, and months of thoughtful follow-up.

Protect your search runway

Spend less runway sorting. Spend more time with owners worth knowing.

  • Get to high-fit owner conversations faster. Start each week with the businesses most likely to justify your time.
  • Personalize with something real. Use company-specific evidence to show an owner why you chose their business.
  • Follow up persistently with the right targets. Save your most expensive attention for owners you would genuinely want to buy from.
  • Let the search sharpen the thesis. Use replies, rejections, and discoveries to improve the next round of scoring.
