● Questions

What people ask before the intro.

If yours isn't here, ask on the call. We'd rather show the work than pitch it.

01What's the pricing?+
Flat monthly retainer, scoped to your batch volume and what we're building for you. No per‑lead pricing, no platform fees. We quote on the intro call once we understand what you're trying to do.
02What do you need from us to start?+
A list of your last 30–150 won deals (company name and close date is enough), and a 60‑minute kickoff. We build a first‑cut ranking, send a small batch, and you tell us where it's right and where it's wrong. You don't need to plug us into your CRM to start.
03How long until we see ranked prospects?+
First batch in week 1–2. Working session in week 3. Monthly delivery starts in week 4. You'll have a ranked pipeline on your data inside a single sprint.
04Do you replace our SDRs?+
No, we feed them. We deliver prospects ranked by fit; your team does the outreach. Most customers see their SDRs get more efficient because they're working a sharper top‑of‑funnel instead of grinding through a noisy list.
05What if we already know who we sell to?+
Good. We treat it as a starting point and put it to the test. The customer you describe on paper often isn't quite the one your team actually closes. We either confirm it fast or find the pockets hiding inside it. Either way, you learn something.
06Where does our data live?+
Our system lives in our infrastructure. We don't take a copy of your CRM, and won‑deal exports stay scoped to the firmographic fields we need.
